Floor Plan Editor · v0.1

Walls

Two ways to define walls — use whichever fits.

Turtle: length direction per line, each wall continues from the last.
Directions: up down left right, or angle 10 @ 45.
move 5 right jumps without drawing.
goto 10,20 teleports cursor to absolute (x,y).

Coordinates: wall x1,y1 to x2,y2 for any single wall.
rect x,y w x h draws a rectangular room (4 walls).
label "Living Room" at x,y places a room label.
Lines starting with # are comments.
